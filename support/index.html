常見問題Frequently asked questions

如何從 Safari 收錄文字？How do I capture text from Safari?

選取文字，點「分享」，再選擇「隨拾句速記」。你可以確認來源、加入標籤與註解後保存。

Select text, tap Share, then choose Quote Hoarder Clip. Review the source, add tags or a note, and save.

在分享選單找不到隨拾句？Quote Hoarder isn’t in the share sheet. What should I do?

先開啟隨拾句一次，再回到分享選單，滑到 App 列尾端點「更多」，將隨拾句加入常用項目。如果仍未出現，請重新啟動 iPhone。

Open Quote Hoarder once. Return to the share sheet, scroll to the end of the app row, tap More, and add Quote Hoarder to Favorites. If it still doesn’t appear, restart your iPhone.

收藏會同步到其他裝置嗎？Do clips sync across devices?

1.1 版只保存在目前裝置，不提供雲端同步。你可以在「設定 → 備份與還原」匯出 JSON 備份。

Version 1.1 stores clips only on the current device and doesn’t provide cloud sync. Export a JSON backup from Settings → Backup & Restore.

如何復原誤刪的收藏？How do I recover a deleted clip?

前往「最近刪除」即可預覽、搜尋與復原 30 天內刪除的收藏，也能一次復原多筆。若確定不再需要，可隨時永久刪除。

Open Recently Deleted to preview, search, and restore clips deleted within the past 30 days. You can restore several at once or permanently remove anything you no longer need.

如何依日期找回收藏？How do I browse clips by date?

開啟日期索引，選擇年份與月份；有收藏的日期會清楚標示。點選月份即可查看當時收錄的所有內容。

Open Date Index and choose a year and month. Dates containing clips are marked, and opening a month shows everything captured then.

如何匯出或還原備份？How do I export or restore a backup?

前往「設定 → 備份與還原」。匯出會建立 JSON 檔；匯入會將備份內容合併至目前收藏。

Go to Settings → Backup & Restore. Export creates a JSON file; import merges that backup into your current collection.

圖片文字辨識會上傳嗎？Are images uploaded for text recognition?

不會。圖片文字辨識在裝置上執行，隨拾句不會把圖片傳送到開發者伺服器。

No. Text recognition runs on-device, and Quote Hoarder doesn’t send images to the developer’s servers.
