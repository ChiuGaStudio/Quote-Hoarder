隱私權政策Privacy Policy

最後更新：2026 年 9 月 24 日Last updated: September 24, 2026

隨拾句（Quote Hoarder）是一款在裝置上保存文字摘錄的工具。本政策說明目前 1.1 版本如何處理資料。

資料處理

隨拾句不要求建立帳號，也不包含廣告、第三方分析或追蹤 SDK。使用者保存的文字、註解、標籤、來源名稱、來源網址與設定，皆儲存在使用者裝置上的 App 與 App Extension 共用容器中，不會由隨拾句傳送到開發者的伺服器。

啟用剪貼簿提醒時，隨拾句會在 App 進入前景時讀取剪貼簿中的文字，以提示是否收錄。文字只有在使用者選擇收錄後才會存入收藏；此功能預設關閉，並可隨時在設定中變更。

從其他 App 使用分享或動作擴充功能時，隨拾句只處理該次由使用者分享的文字、網址、網頁標題或圖片。圖片文字辨識在裝置上執行。分享格式診斷預設關閉；使用者主動開啟後，App 才會在共用容器保留最近一次分享項目的本機診斷資訊。使用者可以查看、關閉並清除診斷，資料不會傳送給開發者。

備份與第三方服務

使用者可以從設定匯出 JSON 備份。備份檔的保存位置與分享對象由使用者選擇，之後的資料處理由所選服務的政策規範。使用者點擊原始網址時，連結會交由系統或使用者選擇的瀏覽器開啟。

資料保留與刪除

收藏會保留在裝置上，直到使用者刪除或移除 App。刪除的收藏會在「最近刪除」保留 30 天，期間可由使用者復原；使用者也可以提前永久刪除。使用者亦可清除分享來源診斷。自行匯出的備份不會隨 App 內刪除或移除 App 而一併刪除，需由使用者在保存位置自行管理。

兒童隱私

隨拾句不會刻意收集任何人的個人資料，也不會要求兒童提供個人資料。

政策更新

若功能或資料處理方式有所變更，我們會更新本頁內容與最後更新日期。

聯絡方式

若對本政策或隨拾句的資料處理有任何疑問，請寄信至 chiugastudio@gmail.com。

Quote Hoarder is an on-device tool for saving text excerpts. This policy explains how version 1.1 handles data.

Data handling

Quote Hoarder requires no account and contains no advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs. Text, notes, tags, source names, source URLs, and settings are stored in a container shared by the app and its extensions on your device. Quote Hoarder does not send this content to the developer’s servers.

If you enable clipboard suggestions, Quote Hoarder reads text on the clipboard when the app enters the foreground so it can offer to save it. Text is stored only after you choose to capture it. This feature is off by default and can be changed in Settings.

When you use a share or action extension, Quote Hoarder processes only the text, URL, page title, or image you choose to share. Text recognition runs on-device. Share diagnostics are off by default. If you enable them, the app stores a local summary of the most recent shared item in the shared container. You can view, disable, and clear it. It is not sent to the developer.

Backups and third-party services

You may export a JSON backup from Settings. You choose where the backup is stored and who receives it; further handling is governed by the selected service. When you open a source URL, the link is handed to the system or your chosen browser.

Retention and deletion

Clips remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Deleted clips stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days and can be restored during that time; you can also remove them permanently sooner. You can clear share diagnostics at any time. Backups you exported separately are not removed when you delete in-app data or remove the app; manage those files at the location you selected.

Children’s privacy

Quote Hoarder does not intentionally collect personal data from anyone and does not ask children to provide personal data.

Changes to this policy

If features or data practices change, we will update this page and its last-updated date.

Contact

For questions about this policy or Quote Hoarder’s data handling, email chiugastudio@gmail.com.
